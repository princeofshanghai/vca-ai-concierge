## Overview

**VCA (Virtual Chat Agent)** is a net-new agentic feature for LinkedIn Premium Company Pages Pro. It adds two coordinated VCA experiences to a Company Page:

- an admin-facing VCA agent for Page owners,
- a visitor-facing VCA agent for Page visitors.

Together, they turn passive Page traffic into actionable intelligence and warm, visitor-initiated conversations.

Target:

- PCP Pro,
- enterprise pilot,
- 10K+ employee companies,
- Q2FY27.

## LinkedIn-Native Insight

Most platforms can tell an admin that someone visited.

LinkedIn can understand who that visitor likely is before they type: role, company, seniority, industry, company size, and signals of professional intent.

VCA is built on that advantage.

## Core Problem

Rose and Cheri are stuck on opposite sides of the same visibility gap.

> Rose cannot see who is there. Cheri will not identify herself.

VCA resolves both sides:

- Rose gets evidence that target buyers are engaging and what they care about.
- Cheri gets a low-friction way to self-qualify before entering a sales funnel.

## Scenario Company

**Velora** is a benefits administration platform. It helps HR teams manage open enrollment, health plans, employee benefits, and carrier complexity in one place.

Velora replaces spreadsheets, disconnected carrier portals, and brittle HRIS workarounds.

The strongest demo story is enterprise open enrollment complexity.

## Admin Persona: Rose Reynolds

Rose Reynolds is the Social Media & Communications Manager at Velora.

Role context:

- reports through brand and communications leadership,
- fully owns the LinkedIn Page,
- owns Page content, strategy, and reporting,
- is separate from demand generation, paid media, and sales development,
- is not responsible for delivering leads to sales.

KPIs:

- follower growth,
- post engagement,
- reactions, comments, and reshares,
- social sentiment,
- website visits from LinkedIn.

Rose's problems:

- She spends time manually synthesizing Page metrics.
- She lacks a benchmark for whether the numbers are good.
- She cannot tell if the right people are showing up on the Page.
- She has limited visibility into what competitors are doing or how Velora compares.
- When leadership asks what LinkedIn is doing for Velora, she has data but no clear story.
- High-intent visitors can land on the Page, leave silently, and she never knows.

Rose's JTBD:

> Prove that organic LinkedIn activity is reaching the right buyers and driving real business value, and get the intelligence to act on it without spending hours in dashboards.

## Visitor Persona: Cheri Sparks

Cheri Sparks is the VP of HR at a 12,000-person retail company.

Situation:

- manages open enrollment across three carriers,
- uses spreadsheets and an outdated HRIS,
- sees the process break every October,
- is quietly evaluating benefits platforms before the next enrollment season.

Cheri's problems:

- Generic company pages only offer marketing copy.
- Contact forms trigger a sales funnel before she is ready.
- She needs to self-qualify quickly without talking to a salesperson.
- She needs enough specifics to begin building an internal business case.

Cheri's JTBD:

> Determine in under five minutes whether Velora solves her specific problem without entering a sales funnel or talking to a salesperson.

## What The Demo Must Prove

- VCA already has useful context before either Rose or Cheri asks a question.
- VCA turns Page analytics into clear recommendations for Rose.
- VCA helps Cheri answer a specific enterprise evaluation question.
- VCA preserves Cheri's agency through a warm LinkedIn message, not a form fill.
- VCA gives Rose a qualified, contextualized conversation only when Cheri chooses to reach out.
