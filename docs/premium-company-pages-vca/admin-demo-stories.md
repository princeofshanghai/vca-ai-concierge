## Story 1A: The Anomaly

Entry point: peek tap to pre-loaded panel.

Rose Reynolds manages Velora's LinkedIn Page on her own: content, strategy, and reporting. She has a leadership meeting at the end of the week and needs to show progress.

She opens her PCP dashboard. Before she does anything, she notices a single prompt floating above the VCA FAB:

> Follower growth dropped this month - find out why.

Rose did not ask. VCA noticed.

She taps it. The panel opens without a greeting or menu and goes straight to the insight:

- follower growth is down 18% month over month,
- posting frequency dropped from three times a week to once over the past two weeks,
- the posting drop is the likely cause.

Rose asks:

> How do my competitors compare?

VCA pulls the competitive layer:

- one key competitor is up 24% in followers this month,
- that competitor is posting enrollment-focused content twice as frequently.

Rose asks:

> What should I do this week?

VCA returns three recommendations:

- post three times this week,
- lead with open enrollment deadline content,
- repurpose Velora's highest-performing post from last month, which drove a spike in Page visits.

Outcome:

> Analytics tell Rose what happened. The VCA agent tells Rose what to do.

## Story 1B: The Contextual Insight

Entry point: inline callout on the Analytics page.

Later that week, Rose opens Analytics to check website visits before a team sync.

At the top of the page, she sees a subtle callout:

> Website visits down 22% this month - may be linked to posting frequency.

She taps it.

The VCA agent opens already oriented to Analytics. Rose does not need to re-explain what she is looking at.

VCA surfaces the correlation:

- website visits dropped during the same two-week posting gap,
- the three posts published before the gap had above-average click-through rates,
- the audience was engaged, but the content stopped coming.

Outcome:

- Rose gets what she needs for the sync in under two minutes.
- The insight comes to her instead of requiring dashboard digging.

## Story 1C: The Leadership Report

Entry point: cold start to prompt click.

Rose has a leadership meeting in an hour. Her manager wants a monthly Page performance summary.

She opens a spreadsheet, then closes it. She opens the VCA agent instead.

The panel opens with the greeting and directional prompts. No anomaly is active; Rose just needs a summary.

Rose clicks:

> How is my page performing this month?

VCA synthesizes across the reporting surfaces:

- follower growth is up 12% month over month,
- two posts on open enrollment deadlines outperformed all other content,
- 68% of Page visitors this month hold HR Director titles or above,
- those visitors match Velora's target buyer,
- one competitor gained followers faster but posted three times as frequently,
- Velora's engagement rate per post is higher.

Outcome:

- Rose has a structured, data-backed summary she did not have to build.
- Total time: under ninety seconds.
- Rose can walk into the leadership meeting with a clear story about organic LinkedIn value.

## Story 1 Acceptance Criteria

- Shows all three admin VCA entry points.
- Demonstrates push and pull prompt logic.
- Keeps Rose's role anchored in brand/comms, not sales development.
- Makes recommendations concrete and tied to observed signals.
- Avoids making VCA feel like a generic blank-input assistant.
